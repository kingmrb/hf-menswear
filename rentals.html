How renting works

No account, no online checkout — it's simple and done in person, the way we've always done it.

1

Find your look

Scroll the styles below and jot down the style number you like.

2

Call or come in

Reserve it with us — just mention the number. Bring your groomsmen or court along.

3

Get measured

We size you and the whole party right here in the store so everything matches.

4

Pick it up

Jim's Formal Wear delivers your rental to the shop, pressed and ready before your event.
